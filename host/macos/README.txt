Cosmic Shore Screensaver {{VERSION}} for macOS
==============================================

The Cosmic Shore Home screen as a Mac screensaver: vessels chasing the crystal through the Garland.
It runs on its own. It does not need the game, an account or the internet, and it sends nothing anywhere.

Needs: macOS 12 Monterey or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.


INSTALL
-------
1. Double-click the zip if your browser did not already open it. You get CosmicShore.saver.
2. Double-click CosmicShore.saver. System Settings asks whether to install it for you or for every
   user of this Mac. Pick one.
3. In System Settings > Screen Saver (on older macOS: Wallpaper > Screen Saver), pick "Cosmic Shore".

  b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
  c. Next to "CosmicShore.saver was blocked", click "Open Anyway" and enter your password.
  d. Double-click CosmicShore.saver again and install it.

Settings (frame rate, which screens, wide or close-up camera) are under "Options..." next to the
screensaver in System Settings.

Move the mouse or press any key to leave the screensaver.

Known macOS issues: since macOS 14 Apple's screensaver host has faults that affect every third-party
screensaver. The small preview in System Settings may look wrong or stay black on macOS 26, and the
Options button can be slow to respond. The full-screen screensaver itself is not affected.


UNINSTALL
---------
In System Settings > Screen Saver, right-click "Cosmic Shore" and choose "Delete". Or delete
CosmicShore.saver from ~/Library/Screen Savers (or /Library/Screen Savers if you installed it for
every user).
